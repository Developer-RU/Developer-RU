### WASH-PRO

I am the **Founder and Lead Architect of WASH-PRO TECH Global**.

WASH-PRO is a hardware and software platform for self-service car washes. I developed the product ecosystem around the equipment, including custom controllers, embedded firmware, displays, communication protocols, payment and RFID integration, backend services and management software.

The project represents my approach to engineering: **the software is not an isolated product — it is part of a complete physical system that has to work reliably in the real world.**
